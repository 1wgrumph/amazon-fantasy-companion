## 1 Introduction

**BLUF:** The companion connects fantasy relevance to a customer-controlled viewing action.

**Frame**

- **Who:** Product implementation and verification teams.
- **What:** Introduction.
- **Why:** Preserve the approved experience and its evidence boundaries.
- **How:** Apply the cited register and model contracts.
- **When:** Phase 2 implementation and subsequent verification.
- **Where:** Fire TV companion and AWS backend.

This is D1, a draft product architecture specification for the Fire TV track and AWS Builder mini challenge. The [gated register](../../docs/plans/2026-10-12-amazon-fantasy-companion-phase-2/requirements-register.md) owns requirement statements; section front matter binds UIDs and generates Appendix A. This document specifies realization and verification approach without replacing the register or claiming the product exists.

The central experience is an alert during third-party viewing, followed by Watch and a supported game/channel handoff. The lineup panel is equally in scope. Unsupported integrations remain visible risks.
