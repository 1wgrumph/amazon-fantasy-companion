## 1 Introduction

The companion is a tune-in alert system that connects fantasy relevance to a customer-controlled viewing action.

This is D1, a draft product architecture specification for the Fire TV track and AWS Builder mini challenge. The [gated register](../../docs/plans/2026-10-12-amazon-fantasy-companion-phase-2/requirements-register.md) owns requirement statements; section front matter binds UIDs and generates Appendix A. This document specifies realization and verification approach without replacing the register or claiming the product exists.

The central experience is an alert during third-party viewing, followed by Watch and a supported game/channel handoff. The lineup panel is equally in scope, with a full-screen companion-app fallback when it cannot sit beside provider video. This preserves the alert and Watch outcome in AFC-PR-015 and AFC-PR-030 without misrepresenting the composition evidence for AFC-PR-041. Section 7 separates document checks from the real-device demonstrations that establish those outcomes.
