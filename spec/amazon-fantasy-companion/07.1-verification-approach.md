### 7.1 Verification approach

**BLUF:** Every UID retains its gated method, case and positive/negative evidence boundary.

**Frame**

- **Who:** Product implementation and verification teams.
- **What:** Verification approach.
- **Why:** Preserve the approved experience and its evidence boundaries.
- **How:** Apply the cited register and model contracts.
- **When:** Phase 2 implementation and subsequent verification.
- **Where:** Fire TV companion and AWS backend.

The following thematic subsections project verification approaches from the [gated register](../../docs/plans/2026-10-12-amazon-fantasy-companion-phase-2/requirements-register.md). Case IDs are VCASE-AFC-PR-NNN; all product cases remain **not executed** in ST-4. A planned test is not a passing result. Preserve register environments and positive/negative criteria when implementing cases; open owner parameters must close before relevant cases can be accepted.

Inspection checks planned implementation structure and configuration. Demonstration checks the real target and customer-observable flow. Tests exercise the contracted data/control behavior. Real-device functional evidence does not become a performance benchmark, and replay labels do not waive actual notification/provider behavior.
