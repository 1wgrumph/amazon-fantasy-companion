### 7.1 Verification approach

Every UID retains its gated method, case and positive/negative evidence boundary.

The following thematic subsections project verification approaches from the [gated register](../../docs/plans/2026-10-12-amazon-fantasy-companion-phase-2/requirements-register.md). Case IDs are VCASE-AFC-PR-NNN; all 63 active product cases remain **not executed** in ST-4. The cases for the three withdrawn UIDs are retired and traced in 4.1.13, not awaiting execution. A planned test is not a passing result. Preserve register environments and positive/negative criteria when implementing cases; the SD-035 parameters are settled, while target and integration evidence must be collected before relevant cases can be accepted.

Inspection checks planned implementation structure and configuration. Demonstration checks the real target and customer-observable flow. Tests exercise the contracted data/control behavior. Real-device functional evidence does not become a performance benchmark, and replay labels do not waive actual notification/provider behavior.
