#### 7.1.6 Policy timing

**BLUF:** Policy timing verification retains the register’s method and case identity.

**Frame**

- **Who:** Phase 2 verification team.
- **What:** Policy timing.
- **Why:** Keep source-to-evidence coverage complete.
- **How:** Execute each named case with its positive and negative criteria.
- **When:** After the allocated behavior is implemented.
- **Where:** The register-specified environment.
