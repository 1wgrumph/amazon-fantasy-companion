#### 4.1.2 Lineup source and scoring

One active lineup source supplies both event matching and scoring, with an explicit scoring context carried to the panel.

`backend.lineup` owns the source switch required by AFC-PR-006 and AFC-PR-007. At T_yahoo (2026-10-16T19:00:00Z), absent Yahoo approval, it accepts manual lineups constructed from ESPN rosters and labels the source manual. Consumers observe one atomic source selection; setup before any configured lineup is not a second active source. Starter changes and source switches invalidate derived values together so alerts and the panel cannot retain conflicting lineup revisions.

AFC-PR-065 places manual scoring in `backend.panelSvc` and `backend.lineup`; its register table is the sole authority for coefficients. The customer selects PPR or Half-PPR through the profile, with Half-PPR as the default, and the panel shows the selected preset name beside the manual source label. AFC-PR-066 makes that choice persistent across restart. Yahoo lineups use their own league scoring settings and never the manual preset table. Keeping source and scoring basis together avoids silently applying a convenient default to a different league.

The cases in 7.1.2 compare computed manual points with the selected register table, exercise a preset change and restart, and check that source switching neither duplicates the active lineup nor applies manual presets to Yahoo.
