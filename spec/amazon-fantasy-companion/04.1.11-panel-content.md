#### 4.1.11 Lineup panel content and remote operation

**BLUF:** The panel is a remote-operated view of lineup, scoring and freshness.

**Frame**

- **Who:** Product implementation and verification teams.
- **What:** Lineup panel content and remote operation.
- **Why:** Preserve the approved experience and its evidence boundaries.
- **How:** Apply the cited register and model contracts.
- **When:** Phase 2 implementation and subsequent verification.
- **Where:** Fire TV companion and AWS backend.

`backend.panelSvc` computes player points, game status, total and progress using the active scoring context; `tvApp.panel` displays the result. Missing values remain unavailable; old values remain stale. The open `stale_after` bound depends on observed feed cadence. A value without a scoring basis is not presented as a confirmed score.

The remote toggles visibility, changes followed team/league and reaches every focusable control with visible focus. Couch legibility is accepted by the owner on the target device under the current register. No numeric font threshold is invented. Hidden state suspends unnecessary display work and refreshes before a newly shown panel is treated as current.
