#### 4.1.7 Alert presentation and commentary

**BLUF:** Presentation preserves the verified trigger context and the customer’s intrusion settings.

**Frame**

- **Who:** Product implementation and verification teams.
- **What:** Alert presentation and commentary.
- **Why:** Preserve the approved experience and its evidence boundaries.
- **How:** Apply the cited register and model contracts.
- **When:** Phase 2 implementation and subsequent verification.
- **Where:** Fire TV companion and AWS backend.

`tvApp.alertSvc` receives profile-relevant updates; `tvApp.notifier` uses the selected presentation. Heads-up includes Watch and Dismiss over third-party playback; panel-only produces no heads-up. The alert retains the triggering player/matchup basis from data even when optional commentary is omitted.

Commentary is backend-generated under the selected excitement level. Event facts and identity are never determined by generated text. Off produces no commentary, and any named entity must be present in the triggering evidence. Voice announcements require both enabled settings and a separately implemented and verified voice route; notification availability does not establish speech capability.
