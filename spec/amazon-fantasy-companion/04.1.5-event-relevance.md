#### 4.1.5 Relevance, identity and corrections

**BLUF:** Canonical event identity governs alert emission and correction propagation.

**Frame**

- **Who:** Product implementation and verification teams.
- **What:** Relevance, identity and corrections.
- **Why:** Preserve the approved experience and its evidence boundaries.
- **How:** Apply the cited register and model contracts.
- **When:** Phase 2 implementation and subsequent verification.
- **Where:** Fire TV companion and AWS backend.

`backend.matcher` reconciles Yahoo, ESPN and provider identifiers before policy decisions. A red-zone event is a team opportunity inside the 20-yard line; it cannot prove a selected player is on the field or will score. Close-game classification waits for the open `close_game_margin` design value.

The event store supplies deduplication identity. An unchanged event produces no new alert, and a scoring correction updates the existing alert record and the panel’s derived state. Distinct entities cannot be merged merely because names resemble each other. Identity ambiguity remains unknown rather than generating confident player commentary.
