## 7 Verification

**BLUF:** D1 verification proves document integrity while product verification remains future work.

**Frame**

- **Who:** Product implementation and verification teams.
- **What:** Verification.
- **Why:** Preserve the approved experience and its evidence boundaries.
- **How:** Apply the cited register and model contracts.
- **When:** Phase 2 implementation and subsequent verification.
- **Where:** Fire TV companion and AWS backend.

ST-4 builds, lints, checks traceability, scans paths and compares a second build byte-for-byte. Those checks establish a reproducible architecture candidate only. Independent review and owner acceptance are separate; no author realization result substitutes for them.

Phase 2 verification follows the register's method and environment per UID. Real-stick demonstrations capture the actual notification, Watch, panel/video and remote behaviors using sanitized evidence under `evidence/visual/`. Hardware benchmarks remain excluded.
