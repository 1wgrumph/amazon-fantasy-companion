#### 7.1.13 Platform and protection

Platform verification inspects the build and deployment declarations and demonstrates operation on the actual target. Configuration claims alone cannot establish device compatibility, Free-plan eligibility or server-side secret containment; each case keeps its distinct evidence boundary.

Table (T1): Platform and protection verification approach

| UID / case | Allocated element(s) | Method, environment and acceptance evidence |

| AFC-PR-052 / VCASE-AFC-PR-052 | SHELL, MANIFEST | Demonstration, tv-app, real 2018 stick (SD-001). Positive: install, launch, and operate on the target. Negative: no target-only failure waived without a C1 owner return. VCASE-AFC-PR-052. |
| AFC-PR-053 / VCASE-AFC-PR-053 | BUILD | Inspection, tv-app, source review of build.gradle.kts. Positive: declaration reads 25. Negative: any other value fails. VCASE-AFC-PR-053. |
