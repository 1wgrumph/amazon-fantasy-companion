### 3.1 Operational concept

**BLUF:** An alert leads to a deliberate Watch action and a verified provider destination.

**Frame**

- **Who:** Product implementation and verification teams.
- **What:** Operational concept.
- **Why:** Preserve the approved experience and its evidence boundaries.
- **How:** Apply the cited register and model contracts.
- **When:** Phase 2 implementation and subsequent verification.
- **Where:** Fire TV companion and AWS backend.

With a linked profile and enabled period, the backend obtains lineup and event facts, matches identities, applies policy and spoiler delay, and sends a relevant alert. The notification exposes Watch and Dismiss during third-party playback when heads-up is selected. Watch respects switching confirmation and invokes the provider contract. Only observed arrival at the intended game/channel establishes the successful handoff case.

Mode B reserves the right side for the lineup panel and fits provider video into the remaining area without crop or overlap. The first-install clock bounds the composition spike to 24 hours. A failure or timeout produces Mode A: the panel beside the companion player with labeled sample footage. This does not redirect Watch to the sample player, waive heads-up behavior, or imply third-party video composition has been solved. Panel visibility is independent of the A/B mode.
