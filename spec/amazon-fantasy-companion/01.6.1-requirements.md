#### 1.6.1 Requirements

Requirement statements remain solely in the gated register.

Read the [gated register](../../docs/plans/2026-10-12-amazon-fantasy-companion-phase-2/requirements-register.md) for normative statements, rationale, allocation and positive/negative criteria. This specification cites UIDs rather than copying statements. Appendix A is generated from section bindings and includes every register UID. Section 7.1 subsections identify the corresponding verification case and approach for each UID. Requirements-engineering owns statement quality; ST-4 does not amend the register.

AFC-PR-049, AFC-PR-050 and AFC-PR-051 remain withdrawn trace entries; all other register UIDs bind active draft scope. The build creates the matrix from section metadata, and set comparison detects either a missing register UID or an invented one.
