#### 4.1.13 Withdrawn requirements: AFC-PR-049, AFC-PR-050 and AFC-PR-051

SD-035 withdraws AFC-PR-049, AFC-PR-050 and AFC-PR-051 because the owner rejected Mode A sample footage.

The UIDs remain bound here so Appendix A explicitly records their withdrawn status. Their footage-license, playback-label and footage-exclusion cases are retired, not pending implementation or verification. No replacement requirement text is created in this specification. AFC-PR-047 and AFC-PR-048 instead govern the full-screen panel and real-provider handoff.

`tvApp.player`, the PLAYER allocation and the native `PanelMode.modeA` element still reflect the withdrawn design. They are retained pending Phase 2 design per SD-035; their presence does not authorize a player implementation or media acquisition. Document verification checks that the three withdrawn UIDs remain in Appendix A and do not appear as active product cases.
