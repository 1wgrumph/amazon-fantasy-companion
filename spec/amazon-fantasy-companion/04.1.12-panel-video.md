#### 4.1.12 Panel consistency and video composition

**BLUF:** Panel mode changes the video composition route while preserving shared facts and real-provider handoff.

**Frame**

- **Who:** Product implementation and verification teams.
- **What:** Panel consistency and video composition.
- **Why:** Preserve the approved experience and its evidence boundaries.
- **How:** Apply the cited register and model contracts.
- **When:** Phase 2 implementation and subsequent verification.
- **Where:** Fire TV companion and AWS backend.

The shell reserves space to the left of the right-side panel and preserves the video aspect ratio; neither overlaying the broadcast nor cropping it satisfies the target. `PanelState.visible` is independent of `PanelMode`.

Alerts and panel state derive from the same lineup/event corrections. Suppressed alerts do not create a fact inconsistency, and a hidden panel refreshes on show. Mode A is the sole modeled fallback transition; returning to Mode B is not specified or silently implemented.
