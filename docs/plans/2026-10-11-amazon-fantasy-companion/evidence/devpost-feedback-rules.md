# Devpost rules extract: product feedback and friction log

Source: https://amazonappdev2026.devpost.com/rules and /resources, fetched 2026-10-12 with curl (HTTP 200). Verbatim text.

## Submission requirements (rules)

Include Product Feedback for each tool, API, or SDK used in your project, tell us: Which developer tools, apis and SDKs did you use and for what? What worked well? (i.e. setup, documentation, docs, testing, performance, reliability) What needs work? (i.e. system errors, docs sections, missing features, compatibility issues, tool limitations, required workarounds) How was your onboarding experience (getting from zero to hello world) Would you build with these devices and services again? Yes/No and please tell us why Identify which Primary Track(s) you are submitting your Project into. Identify which Mini Challenge(s) you are participating in. For AWS Builder (Mini Challenge): Describe which AWS service(s) you used and how, in your Product Feedback answer. For Open Source (Mini Challenge): Provide contribution URL, project repository URL, GitHub username, and a description of what you did, how it works, and why it matters. Optional – Feature Requests: description, why it matters to their project, and priority rating (Critical / Important / Nice-to-have) Optional – Friction Log Entries: specific task attempted, steps taken, expected vs. actual result, severity rating, workaround used, and actionable suggestion. Participants who submit friction logs score higher in judging (up to 10% bonus).

## Bonus points (rules)

Bonus Points: During Stage 1 downselection, Amazon's internal review team assesses each submission's friction log entries (if provided) and passes a recommended bonus — up to 10% — to the Stage 2 judging panel along with the shortlisted projects. The Stage 2 panel applies this bonus to the submission's final spreadsheet score.
